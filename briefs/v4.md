# Redcore v4 addendum — the number v3 asserted but did not compute

**31 August 2026 · appends to Redcore v3 · does not supersede it**

v3 states that registration repeatability, not entropy, is the scarce variable in Brickmark unit identity. That claim was asserted, never computed. Computing it reverses part of the framing.

Accompanying model: `sim/redcore_registration.py`.

## 1. The correct model

Grain-scale magnetization heterogeneity is approximately uncorrelated at the scale of interest. The field at standoff *h* is that source upward-continued: in the Fourier domain, multiplied by `exp(−2πkh)`. Standoff is the low-pass filter that sets how much independent structure exists to measure. Cutoff wavelength ≈ 2πh.

DC is removed. Bulk moment is batch-correlated.

Free parameter: σ_B/n. The pallet study measures it.

## 2. Result — there is less entropy than either review estimated

220 cm² bed face, 1 mm standoff, 3 mm cell pitch:

- raw cells: **2,414**
- genuinely independent cells: **~90**
- extractable after a realistic error budget (0.25 mm lateral, 0.1 mm standoff, σ_B/n = 10, BER ~5%): **~60 bits**

The external review claimed 10⁴ quasi-independent samples. v3 adopted that framing. Both are wrong by roughly two orders of magnitude. Oversampling a smooth field does not create information.

| Standoff | Cutoff λ | Independent cells | Extractable bits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.2 mm | 1.3 mm | 322 | 225 |
| 0.5 mm | 3.1 mm | 174 | 136 |
| 1.0 mm | 6.3 mm | 97 | 55 |
| 2.0 mm | 12.6 mm | 44 | 26 |
| 5.0 mm | 31.4 mm | 15 | 11 |

Common brick roughness is 0.1–1 mm RMS. Sub-0.5 mm standoff on an as-laid face is not achievable without grinding. Practical ceiling on raw common brick: **50–150 bits per face.**

**Derived jig spec:** lateral ≤ 0.25 mm, standoff ≤ 0.2 mm, σ_B/n ≥ 10.

## 3. What this changes

The identity unit is the **course**, not the brick. Four to eight bricks reaches a usable identity length at achievable standoff. Still not cryptographic.

Batch assay is unaffected. Hearthplate is unaffected. The fire test is still the highest-information experiment in the program.

## 4. What v4 does not claim

That the model is validated. That σ_B/n = 10 is achievable. That course aggregation defeats a motivated forger. That any of this makes Brickmark cryptographic.

The property the kiln gives you free is provenance, and there is less of it than either review estimated. It is still probably enough — at the course, not the unit.
