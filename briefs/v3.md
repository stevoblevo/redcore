# Redcore v3 — Provenance, not memory

**Superseding research brief · 31 August 2026**  
Replaces *Redcore — Hematite Lattice Memory* (v1).

v1 asked an ordinary red brick to be a hard drive, a ReRAM array, and a petabyte street. Two adversarial reviews and a fireplace-scale simulation killed those claims. What the kiln actually gives you, for free, is a spatial magnetic map written at firing. That is provenance. The archive that belongs next to a house is a small, self-describing plate a stranger in 2180 can read without our company, our format, or our drive.

See also the [v4 addendum](v4.md), which computes the registration-limited bit count v3 asserted but did not derive.

## 0. What died

- **Bed-face archive glaze.** The face chosen because it disappears into mortar cannot be optically read without demolishing the wall.
- **Brick-body ReRAM at ≤ 3 V.** Forming fields are ~2.6–5 MV/cm (a 10–30 nm gap). Three volts across 65 mm is ~46 V/m.
- **Petabyte-per-façade economics.** LTO-9 is ~$5.50/TB. Density is not the value proposition.
- **The word PUF, used as crypto.** A printed micro-magnet film can reproduce a near-field map at one standoff.
- **Place-binding by embedding bits in the wythe.** A removable plate bound by a hash is software binding. Optical reading is copying.

v1 remains on disk as the document that was attacked. Do not cite its density table or its three-layer brick as current design.

## 1. The free property

Fired red clay records a thermoremanent magnetization as it cools. Red-brick NRM in the literature is typically 0.00075–0.006 Am²/kg, a volume magnetization of order 1–10 A/m, a near-face field of order 1 µT.

Bulk moment is almost worthless as identity. The stochastic signal is the **spatial map**. The scarce resource is **registration repeatability**.

v4 corrects the sample-count framing: at 1 mm standoff a 220 cm² face yields on the order of 90 independent cells and ~60 extractable bits, not 10⁴. The identity unit is the course, not the brick.

## 2. Brickmark — a provenance assay, not a PUF

Adversary: casual substitution and honest error. Not a root of trust.

Two products from one instrument:

- **Batch assay (near-term).** Bulk susceptibility and NRM intensity. Construction materials substitution fraud is a present market.
- **Unit assay (later).** Spatial map, jig, two-standoff scan. Report BER versus cycles and remounts. Aggregate four to eight bricks for a usable identity length.

Jig spec from v4: lateral ≤ 0.25 mm, standoff ≤ 0.2 mm, σ_B/n ≥ 10.

## 3. Hearthplate — a Rosetta object for the 2180 reader

The customer is whoever opens the cassette in 2180.

- Human-readable optical primer at decreasing scale.
- No compression, no encryption in the outer layers.
- Same payload four times. Target: a few hundred megabytes.
- Accessible cassette: lift-off hearth, service chase, or cornerstone. Never the firebox.
- Write cell is institutional (records office).
- Erasure is grinding the plate and photographing the powder.

## 4. Fabric, without wires

No mortar bus. Re-scan the same face. Load-frame test at 0.5–10 MPa. Pass: ΔB at 5 MPa ≥ 3× remount noise.

## 5. Month-one kill criteria

| When | Experiment | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | Ceramic nanolayer on alumina, ASTM E119-class curve + hose-stream | Post-fire BER inside ECC; primer loupe-legible | Film crazes |
| Month 1 | One brick, load frame, 0.5–10 MPa | ΔB at 5 MPa ≥ 3× remount noise | Ratio < 3 |
| Months 1–3 | 100 commons, two batches, two pits | Between-batch > within-batch on bulk χ / NRM | Distributions overlap |
| Months 1–3 | Spectrum at four standoffs (v4) | Matches exp(−2πkh) | Too red; unit assay may be dead |
| Month 6 | Self-describing plate, naive reader | A person who has never seen the spec reads a page | Encoding needs us |

## 6. What we will not claim

That a wall is memory. That 3 V switches a brick. That a façade is a petabyte. That a kiln fingerprint is a cryptographic PUF. That a hash in a header is physical place-binding. That a family should buy this instead of tape. That entropy is abundant. That a year indoors bounds VRM. That service-load strain is readable until the load-frame says so.
